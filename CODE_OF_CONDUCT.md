# Code of Conduct

The RepoLens community welcomes participants across backgrounds, experience levels, and research areas. Discussions should focus on facts, evidence, and reproducible results while respecting other people's time and work.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, discrimination, harassment, publishing private information without consent, and intentionally disrupting collaboration. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation by repeat offenders.

Report incidents through a maintainer's private contact channel and include the context needed to investigate. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and fairly as possible.
